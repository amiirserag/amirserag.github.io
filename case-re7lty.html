01

The Problem

Mobility and travel planning easily fragment into separate tools: one for vehicles, another for places, another for profile or booking activity. The product direction brings those touchpoints into one clearer experience.

02

What I Built

A mobile-first product system covering travel discovery, account/profile concepts, activity views and mobility-oriented flows, with the brand and interface designed to work as one product.

03

The Build

The current product work spans web and app-facing UI, brand exploration and structured product flows. The case study deliberately avoids claiming features as live where they are still in development.

04

Current Status

In development. A public web experience is available while the broader product continues to evolve.
