Venue photography shown from Triple 7's public site. Product UI/screens are intentionally not fabricated here.

01

The Problem

Guests need to understand the venue before arrival: where zones sit, how tables relate to the space, and what the booking path looks like. A static floor plan makes that journey harder to understand and harder to use.

02

What I Built

An interactive venue-map direction that treats the floor plan as a product interface rather than an image. The system is designed around discoverable zones, numbered tables, mobile behavior and a cleaner path into booking.

03

The Build

The work sits inside a wider Triple 7 digital ecosystem that includes venue discovery, booking logic, loyalty concepts and mobile product work. Only verified elements are represented here; no fake performance numbers or invented customer outcomes.

04

Current Status

Active project. The public Triple 7 website is live, while the interactive product work continues as part of the venue's broader digital experience.
